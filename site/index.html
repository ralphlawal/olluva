Questions

Good to know.

Do we both need the app?

Yes. Olluva is a space for two iPhones. One of you invites the other with a code or a link, and from then on it’s just the two of you.

Why is there no account?

Because you shouldn’t need one to leave someone a note. Olluva creates a private identity on your phone. If you’d like to keep your space when you change phones, you can back it up with your Apple ID in Settings.

Can anyone else read our notes?

Not through the app. A space has exactly two members, and our server checks every request against them. Notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted, which is what lets them reach your widget and arrive on schedule; our privacy policy explains this plainly.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Olluva is made for iPhone and its widgets, and both of you will need an iPhone to use it.

Do we both need to pay for Premium?

No. If either of you subscribes, you both get everything in Premium.

What happens if we stop using it?

Either of you can unlink at any time, which closes the space for both of you. You can export what you wrote first, and you can delete everything whenever you like.
